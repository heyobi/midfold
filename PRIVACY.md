# Midfold privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Midfold does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The game runs entirely on your device. Stars, best scores and settings are saved only on the device.
- On iPhone Duo the game reads the hinge angle to turn your pumps into wing flaps. The readings are used live and never recorded or sent anywhere.
- Game Center is optional. If you are signed in, your scores and achievements go to Apple's Game Center under Apple's privacy policy; Midfold itself receives nothing.
- In-app purchases are handled by Apple. Midfold never sees payment details.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The game does not need an internet connection.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/heyobi/midfold/issues
