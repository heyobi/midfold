# Midfold privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Midfold does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- Games run entirely on your device. Best scores are saved only on the device.
- On iPhone Duo the fold games read the hinge angle; on other devices they may read the motion sensor (tilt). These readings are used live to steer the game and are never recorded or sent anywhere.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The app does not need an internet connection.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/heyobi/midfold/issues
